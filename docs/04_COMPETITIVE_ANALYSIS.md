# CanadaCare — Competitive and Existing-Service Analysis

**Status:** Preliminary landscape; update through discovery  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Last updated:** 2026-09-17

## 1. Purpose

Identify existing services and initiatives that overlap with CanadaCare’s proposed patient access, referral, appointment, and coordination features. The purpose is to understand the landscape and avoid duplicating existing capabilities—not to declare that competitors lack features without evidence.

## 2. Landscape categories

| Category | Examples / items to investigate | What to verify |
|---|---|---|
| Hospital patient portals | MyChart and organization-specific portals | Which appointments can be booked, changed, or viewed; scheduling tickets; waitlists; service/eligibility limits. |
| Online appointment booking | Organization-specific booking services | Service coverage, patient eligibility, confirmation/reminders, accessibility, integration. |
| Referral and central intake systems | Ontario Health eReferral / Central Intake initiatives and regional pathways | Participating services/regions, referral tracking, workflow, provider and patient visibility. |
| Diagnostic provider portals | Hospital and community diagnostic imaging portals | Booking access, referral requirements, cancellation processes, geographic/service coverage. |
| Patient record-viewing services | Regional/provincial record access services | Whether they display records or appointments; distinguish record viewing from booking. |
| EMR / HIS / scheduling vendors | Systems used by clinics and hospitals | Existing APIs, integration options, procurement constraints, workflow ownership. |
| Patient communication tools | SMS, email, phone, portal messaging | Consent, identity verification, delivery status, accessibility, auditability. |

## 3. Known examples to verify directly

The following examples were identified in initial research and should be rechecked before external presentation because service features and eligibility can change.

- **Mackenzie Health MyChart:** The organization describes appointment management for some services, including diagnostic imaging; availability and workflow depend on the service.
- **UHN myUHN:** Some clinics use scheduling tickets and some outpatient clinics offer waitlist-style appointment offers; not every clinic necessarily supports the same features.
- **Peterborough Regional Health Centre MyChart / Ontario Breast Screening Program:** Eligible screening participants may have a self-booking route under program criteria; this should not be generalized to all diagnostic imaging.
- **Ontario Health eReferral / Central Intake:** Provincial and regional initiatives address referral workflows for specified pathways. Confirm current rollout, scope, and regional details with official sources.
- **ConnectingOntario / regional record access:** Distinguish clinical information viewing from patient-facing appointment booking.

**Research rule:** Record the exact official page, access date, service, region, eligibility, and observed capability. Do not treat a portal’s existence as proof that all appointments can be booked through it.

## 5. How to compare fairly

For every candidate service, capture:
1. Target users and geography.
2. Supported service lines and appointment types.
3. Whether referral is required.
4. Whether booking is direct, ticket-based, phone-assisted, or staff-managed.
5. Whether rescheduling/cancellation is supported.
6. Whether waitlist offers exist and their eligibility rules.
7. Whether information is patient-facing, provider-facing, or staff-only.
8. Integration standards and documented APIs, if public.
9. Accessibility, language, and non-digital alternatives.
10. Official evidence and date checked.

## 6. Preliminary conclusion

Existing portals and referral initiatives mean CanadaCare should not position itself as “the first app for booking healthcare appointments” or claim that Ontario has no digital referral coordination. A defensible product opportunity, if validated, may involve a narrowly defined coordination problem that current services do not already solve and that participating organizations are willing and able to support.
